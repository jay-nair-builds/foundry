Any fund that files a 13F with the SEC can be added. Enter its name or CIK number.

Submitting opens a pre-filled request on GitHub (free account needed). An automation checks the SEC, adds the investor and rebuilds the site, usually within a few minutes.

Education only. Not investment advice. 13F filings show long US-listed equity positions only, reported up to 45 days after quarter end. They omit shorts, bonds, cash, non-US holdings and any trades since. Copying a portfolio does not copy the investor's timing, sizing discipline or risk limits. Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR.
